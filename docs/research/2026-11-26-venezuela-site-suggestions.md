# Suggested sites and access restrictions in Venezuela

Accessed 2026-11-26. These reports do not establish which sites are blocked
today on a particular network.

- [VE sin Filtro, 2025 censorship report](https://vesinfiltro.org/noticias/2025-censorship-report/),
  published 2026-01-17, documents X/Twitter's named hosts, Signal and Reddit
  restrictions, temporary restrictions on Telegram Web and Canva, and blocks
  of puntodecorte.net and puntodecorte.com. Scope, network and duration vary.
- [Freedom House, Venezuela 2025](https://freedomhouse.org/country/venezuela/freedom-net/2025)
  covers June 2024 to May 2025 and reports widespread media and platform
  restrictions. Its historical findings are not a live availability check.
- [Anthropic supported regions](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/supported-regions)
  and [OpenAI supported countries](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7947663-chatgpt-and-openai-api-supported-countries-and-territories)
  do not list Venezuela. These are provider availability policies, not evidence
  of an ISP block. OpenAI warns that unsupported access may result in account
  suspension; Anthropic's policy also concerns physical location.
- [OpenAI network recommendations](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9247338-network-recommendations-for-chatgpt-errors-on-web-and-apps)
  and [Anthropic network controls](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13198485-enforce-network-level-access-control-with-tenant-restrictions)
  identify relevant service domains, not a complete VPN routing recipe.
- [Chrome forms guidance](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/modern-web-guidance/guides/forms)
  recommends labeled groups and explicit non-submit helper buttons. Suggestions
  append to an editable text area rather than acting as toggles that remove
  user-entered or shared destinations.

The X suggestion includes the hosts listed by VE sin Filtro; Telegram Web,
Reddit, Canva and Punto de Corte use only their identified website names.
This does not cover native apps, arbitrary CDN hosts, voice, blocked DNS, or
all service dependencies. Shared IPs may carry unrelated traffic.
